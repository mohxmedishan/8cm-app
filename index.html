What 8CM actually is

8CM runs on a school day that starts at 8:00 and ends at 2:30 — except Friday, which lets out around 11:30 and is treated accordingly. Thirty students sit in pairs, three pairs to a row, in front of a projector and whiteboard.

The class runs on four houses — Winter, Autumn, Spring, and Summer — plus split groups for Malayalam, Hindi, and French, and shared periods for Islamic Education and Value Education. What holds 8CM together is the batch itself: the friendships, the routines, and the culture built around them.

Real events, achievements, and photos are added as they happen. Until then, you're looking at the foundation.
