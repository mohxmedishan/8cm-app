What 8CM actually is

8CM runs on a school day that starts at 8:00 and ends at 2:30 — except Friday, which lets out around 11:30 and is treated accordingly. Thirty students sit in pairs, three pairs to a row, in front of a projector and whiteboard. Grades 5 through 7 had it easier with LED screens; 8CM made peace with the switch back.

The class runs on four houses — Winter, Autumn, Spring, and Summer — plus split groups for Malayalam, Hindi, and French, and shared periods for Islamic Education and Value Education. Sarvesh currently holds the prefect seat, after Khush's multi-year run in the role. On the field, football is the sport everyone actually shows up for.

Real events, achievements, and photos are added as they happen. Until then, you're looking at the foundation.
