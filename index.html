What CM actually is

CM is bigger than any one grade. It's the thread connecting the different classes, the friendships that carry forward, the inside jokes that somehow survive every timetable change, and the memories built together year after year. Right now, this site is documenting the 8CM batch as the latest chapter.

Within the current batch, thirty students sit in pairs, three pairs to a row, across four houses — Winter, Autumn, Spring, and Summer — with split groups for Malayalam, Hindi, and French, plus shared periods for Islamic Education and Value Education.

The site grows with CM. Older batches, shared memories, achievements, and class history can be added without changing what CM is at its core.

What changed, what stayed.

Grades 5–7 had LED screens. The 8CM classroom moved back to a projector and whiteboard. Same room, different setup, same people making it theirs.

School day

8:00 to 2:30, mostly.

The regular school day runs from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM. Fridays finish around 11:30 AM, which naturally becomes a whole different species of school day.
